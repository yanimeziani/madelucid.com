How it works

Three steps. You do two.

01

Script

It knows you. A short interview fills your Me file, and every script starts from it: your stories, your words, a hook that earns the next second.

02

Teleprompter

Read it to the camera. Restart a line as often as you like; the last clean take wins and the dead air goes.

03

Magic

Cut, framing, kinetic type, sound design and music in a few versions. Watch the storyboards, pick one, get the final. Nothing posts without your OK.

Before you open it

It's a beta. Two clicks more.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Madelucid into Applications.
  2. Open it once. macOS says it can't check the developer: that's expected, the beta isn't notarized yet.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.
  4. The first run installs what the edit needs (ffmpeg, the engine, music and sound effects). About 2.5 GB, ten minutes or so. Once.

You'll need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13+
  • About 6 GB free
  • Claude Code or Codex, signed in. Setup installs Claude Code if you don't have it.
  • A camera. Your phone works: drop the takes in.
